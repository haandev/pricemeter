## Price table

<Source file="examples/saas-seats/src/billing.ts" decl="PRICE_ROWS" />

Scopes are `account:{id}` > `plan:{plan}:{currency}` > `list:{currency}`. EUR accounts read EUR rows; the `Rate` itself has no currency (B17). The first 10 GB are included.

## Catalog and `getRate`

<Source file="examples/saas-seats/src/billing.ts" from="const table = layeredRates" to="^    \.commit\(" />

A seat's price is its monthly price per cycle; because the quantity is seat-seconds, `getRate` sets every tier's `per` to the cycle's seconds. A seat added halfway costs half (B9). `usedSoFar` is read over the cycle window.

## Closing a cycle

<Source file="examples/saas-seats/src/billing.ts" from="async closeCycle(" to="^    \}," />

1. `integrate` reduces level samples to two quantities: seat-seconds (`time_weighted`) and peak GB (`max`, B10).
2. One multi-line `observe` with a period ref (`{account}:{cycle key}`) and `at: cycle.to − 1` (A98).
3. `minimumCommit` tops the enterprise plan up to its monthly minimum (B12), written with `metering.commit(plan)`.

Both steps are idempotent: a cron that closes the same cycle twice writes nothing twice.
