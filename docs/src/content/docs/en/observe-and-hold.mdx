`observe`, `hold`, `extend`, `capture` and `release` live on the bound catalog. Each expands lines (`feeds`), calls `getRate`, runs `price()`, commits and returns a result. To see the plan, call `metering.plan.<same call>`: same arguments, no `commit`, returns `{ result, plan, lines }`.

## `observe`

Two signatures: one line and many lines.

```ts
metering.observe(meter, dims, quantity, ref, ctx, opts?)
metering.observe([{ meter, dims, quantity }, …], ref, ctx, opts?)
```

A multi-line `observe` is one consistent observation: one plan, one `commit`, all or nothing. `quantity` is a non-negative integer; the unit is your choice (messages, tokens, minutes, seat-seconds, a transaction amount in micro-USD).

| Option | Meaning | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `at` | Observation time (epoch ms); passed to `getRate` and written on usage rows | `Date.now()` |
| `usedSoFar` | Overrides `getRate`'s; a number for one line, `{ [meter]: number }` for many | — |
| `ifMissing` | When `getRate` returns `null`: `reject` (write nothing, `no_price`) or `free` (count and write at 0) | `reject` |
| `feeds` | `false`: skip pool expansion | `true` |

## Three states

| State | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A tariff with price 0 | Free **and** priced: counted, 0 written to usage, nothing to the ledger |
| `getRate` → `null`, `ifMissing: "reject"` | `{ ok: false, reason: "no_price", meter }`; nothing written |
| `getRate` → `null`, `ifMissing: "free"` | Counted and written with a zero-priced one-tier tariff |

`no_price` is a state, not an error: an item whose price is published late (such as WhatsApp window prices) can be queued and retried with the same `at` ([messaging example](/pricemeter/en/examples/messaging/)).

## Hold: a value object

<Source file="docs/src/snippets/plan-and-hold.test.ts" region="hold" />

- A `hold` is a multi-line reservation. Each line's tariff and `usedSoFar` are **embedded in the hold**; the application stores it (e.g. on the OTP record) and the library never reads the ledger.
- Every call returns the **updated** hold (`result.hold`, A87). Pass the new value to the next call. Retrying with the same old hold produces the same `refId` and is a no-op in `commit`.
- The reservation is each line's [hold upper bound](/pricemeter/en/rate-models/#the-hold-upper-bound); pool lines are reserved too. A hold returns `charged: 0`: a reservation is not a money movement.
- A hold has **one line per `(meter, dims)`** (A101): lines with the same key merge, and a pool line carries its feeders as `feeders[]`. The hold rounds each key once: under `cumulative` it equals an `observe` with separate lines exactly; under `per_event_up` it differs only by rounding (at most 2 micro-units per line), in either direction.
- `extend` grows the hold: existing `(meter, dims)` lines grow on their stored tariff, `getRate` is called only for new lines, and pool lines follow their feeders.
- `capture` charges what actually happened. The line is found by `meter`; when the same meter is on several lines, `dims` is required. Pool lines are captured along with their feeders. Partial and repeated captures are fine.
- `release` frees the remaining reservation (`upperBound − captured`) and closes the hold.

## Telescoping captures

<Source file="docs/src/snippets/plan-and-hold.test.ts" region="telescoping" />

Partial captures round the hold as one observation: the n-th capture charges `R(u, c+q) − R(u, c)`, where `u` is the `usedSoFar` at hold time and `c` the units captured so far (A88). Otherwise `per_event_up` would push piecewise captures over the upper bound.

## Capture-time tariff

<Source file="docs/src/snippets/plan-and-hold.test.ts" region="capturerate" />

By default a capture uses the tariff stored in the hold and does not call `getRate` again: an open hold keeps the old price even after a custom price ends (B20). To price at capture time, pass `{ rate }`: one tariff or `{ [meter]: Rate }`. The total still cannot exceed the hold's upper bound.

## `hold_exceeded` and `hold_closed`

<Source file="docs/src/snippets/plan-and-hold.test.ts" region="closed" />

`hold_exceeded`: a line would capture more units than reserved, or the total amount would exceed the upper bound. `hold_closed`: the hold was released (A86). Neither writes anything; `extend` first.

## Result reasons

Success: `{ ok: true, charged, lines }` (holds and captures add `holdId` and `hold`; holds add `upperBound`). `charged` is this call's net money movement: charges, captures and adjustments, not reservations.

| `reason` | When | Written |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `no_price` | `getRate` returned `null` and `ifMissing` is `reject` | nothing |
| `usage_required` | The tariff needs `usedSoFar` and none was given | nothing |
| `insufficient_credit` | `commit` threw `InsufficientCredit` (also matched by name, so it survives RPC) | nothing |
| `quota_exceeded` | `usedSoFar` + requested > the `limit` from `getRate`, or `commit` threw `QuotaExceeded`; `cause: { meter, limit, used, requested }` | nothing |
| `invalid_context` | The context failed its schema, `accountId` is not a string, or the hold belongs to another account | nothing |
| `invalid_dims` | The dimensions failed the meter's schema | nothing |
| `invalid_rate` | A tariff from `getRate` or capture `{ rate }` failed `validateRate` | nothing |
| `invalid_ref` | The `ref` is malformed or `ref.type` is not in `refs()` | nothing |
| `hold_exceeded` | The capture exceeds the reservation or the upper bound | nothing |
| `hold_closed` | The hold was already released | nothing |
| `commit_failed` | `commit` threw anything else; the error is in `cause` | up to `commit`'s atomicity: should be nothing |

<Aside type="caution" title="Exceptions, not values">
Programmer errors throw instead of returning a `Result`: a meter not in the catalog, a negative or non-integer `quantity`, a capture line that is not in the hold or is ambiguous (no `dims`), an invalid `feeds` weight, and `observe` on a catalog without bound adapters (TypeScript already rejects that at compile time).
</Aside>

## The balance gate

“Balance ≥ amount” is not in the library. `commit` throws `InsufficientCredit` when short and the library returns `insufficient_credit`. Holds and extends count as debits too: a prepaid `commit` refuses a reservation beyond the available balance. Postpaid means `commit` never throws (B26).

## Quotas

The balance gate answers "can they pay?"; a quota answers "are they allowed?". Next to `rate` and `usedSoFar`, `getRate` can return a `limit` for the same period (A107):

<Source file="docs/src/snippets/plan-and-hold.test.ts" region="quota" />

- **All or nothing.** At 9/10 a request for 4 is refused entirely. Partial use is the app's call: `cause.limit − cause.used` is what's left.
- **Per meter.** Lines of the same meter add up, whatever their dims. `limit: 0` means the meter is not in the plan (unlike `no_price`: priced, but not allowed).
- **Pools.** A limit on a pool meter caps every channel that feeds it ("SMS + WhatsApp 1,000 in total, then stop").
- **Atomic.** The library's early check can't see two concurrent requests that both read 999. So the limit is written on the usage row and the adapter re-checks it inside `commit`'s transaction, throwing `QuotaExceeded` ([adapter contract](/pricemeter/en/adapters/#the-commit-contract)).
- **Holds.** `hold` and `extend` check the reserved quantity; `capture` is never refused (a sent SMS must be recorded).
